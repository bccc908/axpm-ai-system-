# 合规与风控 / Compliance and Risk

Use this folder for approved compliance and risk rules only.

Suggested documents to add:

- Required disclaimers
- Claims staff and bots must not make
- Risk review rules
- Restricted jurisdictions or client types, if any

Template: document title, owner, last updated date, then the approved text. If a rule is missing, say it is not documented. Do not invent a compliance position.
